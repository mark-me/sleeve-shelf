# Code of Conduct

Sleeve & Shelf is a small project, and the aim is simple: that everyone who reports a bug, suggests an idea or sends a change is treated with respect.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, also when you disagree.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before you conclude.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Keep in mind that this is one person's spare-time project: answers may take a while.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminating remarks or jokes about anyone's identity or background.
- Sharing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a friendly, professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else someone represents the project.

## Reporting and what happens next

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainer, [mark-me](https://github.com/mark-me) on GitHub. Reports are treated in confidence. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone who does.
